# 🏫 Institute Maintenance Portal

The **Institute Maintenance Portal** is a centralized web-based system designed to streamline the reporting, allocation, and resolution of maintenance issues within an educational institution.

It provides a structured and transparent workflow that improves communication between complainants, administrators, and technicians, ensuring faster and more efficient issue resolution.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Managing maintenance complaints manually or through scattered communication channels often leads to delays, lack of accountability, and poor tracking. This portal solves these challenges by offering a unified digital platform for complaint management.

---

## 🚀 Features
- Centralized maintenance complaint registration  
- Category-based issue reporting (Electrical, Plumbing, Computer, Laboratory, etc.)  
- Role-based access control  
- Task allocation and monitoring by the Principal (Admin)  
- Real-time status updates by technicians  
- Transparent complaint tracking for complainants  

---

## 👥 User Roles & Workflow

### 🔹 Complainant
- Submits maintenance issues through the portal  
- Tracks real-time status updates of complaints  

### 🔹 Principal (Admin)
- Reviews submitted complaints  
- Assigns tasks to appropriate technicians  
- Monitors progress and resolution  

### 🔹 Technician
- Receives assigned maintenance tasks  
- Updates complaint status in real time  
- Marks issues as resolved after completion  

---

## 🔄 System Workflow
1. Complainant registers a maintenance issue  
2. Principal reviews and assigns the issue to a technician  
3. Technician works on the task and updates its status  
4. Complainant receives real-time updates until resolution  

---

## 🎯 Benefits
- Faster maintenance issue resolution  
- Improved accountability and transparency  
- Efficient task management  
- Reduced manual paperwork and miscommunication
